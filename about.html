Our Journey

How We Got Here

Every milestone shaped who we are and how we serve our clients today.

2016 — Founded
The Beginning
MNH Advertising was founded in Karachi with a small team and a big vision — to help local businesses grow through creative marketing and modern digital tools.
2018 — Expansion
Building Our Portfolio
We expanded our services to include social media management, paid advertising, and full brand identity creation — growing to serve 40+ clients across multiple industries.
2021 — Growth Phase
Going Full Digital
Embracing the digital revolution, we launched data-driven campaign services, performance marketing, and influencer strategy — helping brands scale in a post-pandemic world.
2024 — Today
150+ Brands. One Mission.
With a team of 12+ specialists and a proven track record across 150+ brands, MNH Advertising is now one of Karachi's most trusted creative digital agencies — and we're just getting started.
